Frontier Plan: crypto, hard assets, energy and space

The Goal-25 framework rebuilt around Bitcoin, gold and silver, miners, metals, uranium, energy, aerospace and space, with SpaceX as the core growth asset. Seven sleeves in four risk groups, levered to a 40% volatility target with hard caps on crypto.

Backtest October 2015 – November 2026, monthly, net of trading costs and borrowing. The target was chosen on 2015–2020 and tested unchanged on 2021–2026. Not investment advice.

SpaceX: the core growth asset

SpaceX (SPCX) since its listing

  • Five months can't be backtested. The space sleeve uses a chain of public proxies, equal-weighting whatever existed on each day:
    • ITA and XAR aerospace ETFs (2006+);
    • UFO and ROKT space ETFs (2019+);
    • ARKX and Rocket Lab (2021+);
    • XOVR and DXYZ, funds that held pre-IPO SpaceX shares (2024+);
    • SPCX itself from June 2026.
  • The sleeve holds full size above its 50-day average and half size below. SpaceX's volatility since listing (about 80% a year) is close to Bitcoin's, so size it like crypto, not like an index fund.

The plan

Sleeves and sizing (× account value)

SleeveGroupSharpeNowAvg

Hard caps: BTC ≤ 0.35×, ETH ≤ 0.15×, VIX spike fade ≤ 0.25×, gold put-spread sleeve ≤ 0.5×. Total exposure averaged about 2.6×.

How it is run

  • Crypto: hold BTC and ETH only while each is above its own 50-day average, otherwise cash. The exit avoided most of 2018 and 2022.
  • Hard-asset rotation: each month, hold the top 3 of gold, silver, miners, metals, copper, uranium, energy, oil services, platinum, aerospace and space by strength rating. Each is held only while above its own 50-day average. Aerospace, junior gold miners and copper were held most often.
  • Trend: long or short gold, silver, base metals, oil, commodities, energy, miners and BTC, each sized to 10% volatility.
  • Option premium: each month, sell a gold put spread and spend the credit on gold calls (priced from the gold volatility index), plus the VIX spike fade.
  • Each Friday: give the four groups equal risk, then scale the book to a 40% volatility target, capped at 6×.

Rolling-window optimisation

Every January, the walk-forward version re-picks the weighting method, volatility target (20–40%) and leverage cap (3×, 4× or 6×) from the previous 3 years only. It chooses the highest trailing return with a drawdown no worse than −30%.

YearWalk-forward picked

Here re-optimising came close to the fixed plan: 26.5% against 28.1% a year in 2021–2026. That's because it kept picking the same 35–40% targets. As with Goal-25, it never beat the fixed setting, so fix the settings.

  • Against QQQ, SPY and gold: large and statistically clear alpha (t-stats of 3–3.5). The plan captured more of their up months than their down months.
  • Against Bitcoin: the plan made less (−24% a year) but with a third of BTC's risk. It caught only 22% of BTC's down months. Owning BTC outright paid more in the boom, but lost 75% in 2018 and 64% in 2022.
  • Diversification is real: crypto, hard-asset trend, the gold option sleeve and the VIX fade are nearly uncorrelated (mostly below 0.3). That's why the unlevered core reached a Sharpe of 1.24 over the full period and 0.93 in 2021–2026.

What to expect live: about 18–25% a year. That is based on the 2021–2026 test results with a 0–25% Sharpe discount. Losing years of −17% (2018, 2022) and drawdowns of about −30% should be expected. A 25%+ year came in roughly half of the simulated 5-year paths.

Risks and how to run it

  • Crypto crash days: a day like 12 March 2020 (BTC −40%, ETH −45%) costs about 18–21% of the account at the capped sizes. Never lift the crypto caps.
  • Metals crash days: the worst day in the backtest was 30 January 2026 (−17%), when gold and silver collapsed together. The gold option sleeve, the rotation and the trend sleeve all lost at once.
  • SpaceX has five months of trading history. It has already fallen 49% from peak at times, and its volatility is about 80% a year. Treat the space sleeve as high-risk growth until it has a longer track record.
  • Practical tools:
    • spot BTC/ETH ETFs (IBIT, ETHA) or CME micro futures for crypto;
    • ETFs for the rotation and space sleeves;
    • micro futures (MGC, SIL, MCL, MES) for trend;
    • GLD listed options for the put-spread sleeve;
    • a capped SVXY position for the spike fade.
  • Modelled option prices, free data and a short history (11 years): paper-trade it first.
