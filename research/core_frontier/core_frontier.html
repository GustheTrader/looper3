    3 · Build it in four steps

    1

    Fund the core (day 1)

    Buy the core in your chosen proportion, but only the holdings whose EMA signal is on today (see "Signals today"; a holding whose signal is off waits in T-bills): Bitcoin and Ethereum in spot (or spot ETFs), the hard-asset and space ETFs, and the gold put-spread sleeve. Set each position by the EMA signal: full size when price is above all four EMAs (20, 50, 100, 200), proportionally less with each one it loses, cash when below all four. Needs: a brokerage account with ETFs and options approval for defined-risk spreads, plus a crypto venue.

    2

    Add the carry and momentum sleeves (week 1)

    Funding carry needs spot crypto plus a perpetual-futures account on a regulated or large venue; split it across at least two venues. Momentum long/short needs a margin account that can short. Both are market-neutral, so they can go on at any time.

    3

    Arm the event sleeves (weeks 2–4)

    The VIX spike fade and the FF calendars wait for a signal, so there may be nothing to trade for weeks. Set the alerts (VIX, VIX3M, VVIX and the Forward Factor on SPY, QQQ, IWM and DIA), write the rules on the desk sheet below, and keep the cash for them in T-bills.

    4 · The rules

    Core: EMA ensemble

    Entry and size

    Hold each asset in proportion to how many of its 20, 50, 100 and 200-day EMAs it is above, decided at the prior close. Cash below all four.

    Limits

    Four equal-risk groups; 40% volatility target; 6× gross cap; Bitcoin ≤ 0.35×, Ethereum ≤ 0.15×.

    Why it works

    VIX spike fade

    Entry

    VIX ≥ 25, the VIX curve back in contango (VIX below VIX3M), and VIX at least 15% below its 10-day high. Short 0.5× the sleeve in 30-day VIX futures, or the equivalent in SVXY.

    Exit

    VIX below 18, 40 days elapsed, or VIX back above VIX3M.

    Guardrails

    Stand aside if VVIX is above 120. Short-VIX exposure never above 10% of the account; a day when VIX doubles then costs about of the portfolio.

    FF calendars

    Entry

    Forward Factor above 0.20 on SPY, QQQ, IWM or DIA (FF = (front IV − forward IV) / forward IV, from the ~30-day and ~90-day at-the-money vols). Sell the front-month at-the-money call, buy the same strike about 90 days out, as one order.

    Size

    4 / 6 / 8 / 10% of the FF sleeve per calendar when VIX is below 20 / 20–25 / 25–30 / above 30. Keep 20% of the sleeve in cash.

    Exit

    Close when FF falls below 0.15, or 7 days before the front expiry. Do not hold to expiry.

    Funding carry

    Entry

    Per coin (50% Bitcoin, 50% Ethereum): buy spot and short the same notional of the perpetual while its trailing 7-day funding is more than 2% a year above T-bills, judged on the prior day.

    Exit

    Funding falls below that line: close both legs and hold T-bills. Each switch costs about 0.2%.

    Guardrails

    The price risk cancels; the exchange risk does not. No more than half the sleeve on one venue.

    Momentum long/short

    Rule

    Long the top decile of US stocks by 12-month momentum, short the bottom decile, scaled to 12% volatility.

    Role

    Market-neutral; it moves independently of the core and the VIX sleeve.

    Working every option order

    1. One combo order for the whole spread. Never leg in.
    2. Start at mid; improve by 10% of the bid/ask width every 5 minutes; never pay more than mid + 30% of the width.
    3. Trade 10:00–15:30 New York time.
    4. No fill by 15:30: cancel and re-quote tomorrow if the signal still holds.
    5. Once filled, place the exit as a good-till-cancelled limit.

    Review triggers (policy, not backtested)

    • Portfolio 10% below its peak: check that every sleeve is behaving to rule and that the split is still within 5 points of target.
    • Portfolio 20% below its peak: reduce to the next lower risk level until it recovers to within 10% of the peak.
    • A sleeve 2× its worst backtest drawdown: halve it and re-examine it before adding back.
    • Margin use above 70%: cut leverage; above 80%, de-lever at once.
