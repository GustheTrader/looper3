Core Frontier Investment Portfolio

65% core investments (crypto, hard assets, energy and space, with SpaceX as the core growth asset) and 35% short-term trading sleeves: VIX strategies, Forward Factor calendars, momentum long/short and prediction-market models. The trading sleeves are chosen to make money when the core is losing it.

Backtest October 2015 – November 2026, monthly, net of trading costs and borrowing. 2021–2026 is the untouched test period. Not investment advice.

Trading sleeves (35%)

VIX spike fade · 15%

  • Rule: short VIX futures (0.5× the sleeve) only after a spike has peaked. That means VIX ≥ 25, the term structure back in contango, and VIX 15% below its 10-day high. Exit below VIX 18, after 40 days, or the moment VIX ≥ VIX3M again.
  • Why it fits: it sells volatility when premiums are richest, after the crash rather than during it. Over 2021–26 it earned about 12% a year (Sharpe 0.67). The same rule on the real SVXY fund (2011+) matched the model.
  • Guardrails from the Principled Trading Group deck: trade only inside the VIX 16–30 band, stand aside if VVIX tops 120, and keep VIX structures to 2–5% risk each. A VIX-doubling day costs about 7.5% of the whole portfolio at this size.

Momentum long/short · 5%

  • Rule: long the top 10% of US stocks by 12-month momentum and short the bottom 10%, scaled to 12% volatility (survivorship-free CRSP data).
  • Why it fits: it is market-neutral and uncorrelated with the core and the VIX sleeve. It earned about 7% a year in 2021–26.

Prediction models · 5% (pilot)

15-min BTC up/down markets, last 60 daysTradesWonAvg priceEdge / contractt-stat

This tests the deck's Keltner-channel dip-buy on BTC itself. It assumes the market prices "Up" at fair random-walk odds. Dips that reach the Keltner band finished above the open more often than those odds imply, while ordinary dips did not. The 1-hour trend filter cut the sample without adding edge. Paper-trade against real Polymarket prices before funding this sleeve.

FF calendars · 10% (pilot): Forward Factor over time

FF = (front IV − forward IV) / forward IV, from the 30-day (VIX) and 93-day (VIX3M) curves. The dashed line is the 0.20 entry threshold from the FF deck. For the S&P 500, FF was above 0.20 on only 2% of days.

The FF deck's crisis results did not reproduce. With your deck's rules (FF > 0.20, 4–10% sizing by VIX band, exit at FF < 0.15), the calendars lost about 3% a year and won 23% of trades. March 2020 came out at −3%, not +43–62%. Calendars are short the front option, so they lose when prices swing hard, and crises are when prices swing hardest. With bid/ask kept at 2% even in panics, the result is about break-even (+1.5% a year, March 2020 +17%), so execution decides everything. Keep FF calendars at pilot size, as the deck's own risk slide warns, until live fills confirm an edge. They are also more promising on single names, where FF rises above 0.20 far more often than on the index.

What to expect live: about 15–21% a year, with drawdowns near −20%. The 65/35 split trades return for safety. Over 2021–26 it made 21% a year against 27% for the core alone, but its worst drawdown was −19% against −33%, and its worst year was −3% against −11%. Raising the core's risk target didn't recover much return, because the crypto caps bind. If 25%+ is the priority, the lever is a bigger core share, not more leverage.

Operating rules

  • Rebalance to 65/35 monthly, or when either side drifts more than 5 points.
  • Margin: keep portfolio-margin use under 70% and de-lever at 80%. Keep at least 20% of each trading sleeve in cash (from the PTG and FF decks).
  • Per-trade risk:
    • VIX structures: 2–5%;
    • FF calendars: 4–10% by VIX band (pilot: use half);
    • prediction trades: 0.5–1%, at most 5 open, −3% daily loss limit, 24-hour halt after a 5% sleeve drawdown.
  • Stress budget:
    • a March-2020 style crypto crash day costs about 13.5% of the portfolio at the capped sizes;
    • a VIX-doubling day costs about 7.5%.
    Never lift the crypto or VIX caps.
  • Promote a pilot sleeve (FF calendars, prediction models) to full size only after 3 months of live or paper results that match the backtest.
